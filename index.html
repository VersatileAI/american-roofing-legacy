QUESTIONS WE GET · STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Ask us anything.

These are Andrew's answers. If yours isn't here, call — it's quicker than reading.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes, our inspections are free.

Should I call my insurance company or a roofing company first after a storm?

Call for a roof inspection and then after seeing the inspection report your contractor can help you determine if you should call your insurance company to make a claim.

What are the signs of hail damage on a roof?

Common signs include bruising or dents on shingles, missing or damaged granules, cracked or split shingles, and exposed areas where the shingle surface has been impacted. Hail can also damage metal flashing, vents, gutters, downspouts, siding, windowsills, and other exterior surfaces.

Can my roof have storm damage even if I cannot see it from the ground?

Yes, depending on the height and pitch of the roof, many areas may not be visible from the ground.

How quickly can you come out for an inspection?

Usually within 1-2 days, weather permitting.

How long does it take to replace a roof?

Usually 1-2 days depending on the size of the roof.

How much does a roof replacement typically cost?

The cost of a roof replacement depends on several factors, including the size and pitch of the roof, roofing material selected, number of existing layers, roof design and complexity, accessibility, and whether repairs to the roof decking or other components are needed. For that reason, there is no single price that applies to every home. Two houses with similar square footage can have very different roofing costs depending on the design and condition of the roof.

Do I have to use the roofing company recommended by my insurance company?

No, you can use the contractor or company of your choice.

Can you meet with my insurance adjuster?

Yes, we do that.

Are impact-resistant shingles worth the extra cost?

For many homeowners in North Texas, impact-resistant shingles are worth considering, particularly because hail is a significant concern in our region. Their enhanced resistance to impact can help reduce the likelihood of certain types of hail damage and may provide additional peace of mind. The value depends on several factors, including the additional cost of the shingles, the expected life of the roof, your property's exposure to severe weather, and whether your insurance company offers a premium discount for qualifying impact-resistant roofing. It's important to compare the actual cost difference and potential insurance savings rather than assuming the upgrade will pay for itself. Impact-resistant shingles can still be damaged by severe hail, so they should be viewed as a more durable roofing option, not a guarantee against storm damage.

Do you offer workmanship warranties?

Is American Roofing registered and insured?

Yes, we are a registered contractor in the cities that we work in and we are fully insured.

What should I do if my roof is actively leaking?

Call us for an inspection and estimate to make the repairs. We can temporarily cover a leaking roof until repairs can be completed.
